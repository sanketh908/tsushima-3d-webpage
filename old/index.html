Bun'ei invasion

1274

  1. In the tenth month of 1274, a Yuan–Goryeo fleet of some nine hundred ships sails out of Korea.
  2. Its first landfall is Komoda beach, on the island of Tsushima.
  3. The island's deputy governor, Sō Sukekuni, rides out to meet it with about eighty horsemen.
  4. None of them come back. The fleet moves on to Iki, then to Hakata Bay.
  5. Nearly seven and a half centuries later, Sucker Punch Productions turned that beach into Ghost of Tsushima (2020).
